  **Welcome to the Deep Learning Workshop for 2026.**

  We meet on two Saturdays, **21 and 28 November, 10:00-18:00**, in **Room 3.30, Hertie School (Friedrichstraße 180)**. Each day has approximately three sessions: 10:00-12:30, 13:30-15:00 and 15:30-18:00 - these are not hard and fast, rather we'll take breaks as needed.

  To get the materials, join the course from the **Join** page: it takes a minute and you need a GitHub account.

  The sessions are envisioned as **interactive lectures**, with primarily theoretical content being delivered on the day. Additionally, I provide **handouts** and **notebooks** for further, more practical (i.e. code-implemented) self-study. The theoretical in-person sessions should set you up well to engage with the notebooks, and the slides have frequent code snippets throughout to link theory to code.

  Fair warning: this workshop will **move fast** and aims to cover a lot of ground in a short space of time. Come to it as a **collaborative challenge**! Together we are going to see what we can get through in a very compressed amount of time. It is **not** expected you will leave the workshop with full understanding of the full field of deep learning, a working memory of each architecture or concept you might need in your own research, and complete fluency with PyTorch and Transformers libraries. Rather **success** looks like you having a better understanding of some of the core concepts of deep learning - what it is, how it works, when it works, and when it doesn't (and why not) - and knowing where to look yourself to reinforce, deepen and extend from there.
